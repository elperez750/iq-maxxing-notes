**Genre:**  Biography
**Period covered:**  1386-1422

## Overview
*1-2 sentence summary of what this book is actually about*

###### This book is primarily about the life of King Henry V and his rise to power. It is split into two sections; Prince, and King, both which tell a very distinct story. 


---

## Key Figures
*For each major person:*
- **[[Henry V Person]]** — This is the main character of the story. He is the warrior king who did many things
- **[[Henry IV]]** — This is the father of prince Henry. He overthrew the previous [[King Richard II]]
- **Richard II** — This is the man who was king before Henry. Ruthless, childish, and will do not stop to get revenge
- **John of Gaunt** — This is the grandfather of Henry. He is a patron of writers including [[Geoffrey Chaucer]]. He is a soldier, a diplomat, and the protector of radical theologians such as John Wyclif.
- **Thomas Mowbray** — One of the appellant's; he is the one who is about to duel Henry IV before the fight is stopped.
- **Thomas of Lancaster** — Brother of Henry V
- **Thomas Arundel** — The former archbishop of Canterbury, who was banished in 1397 by the 1397 parliament that executed his brother.

###### This is a chapter where Henry learns about how kings maintain power.  King Richard goes to France to marry Charles VI's daughter.

>pg. 15
>"Boys like Henry represent the future. They are also bargaining chips. Indeed, talk of arranging a weeing for Henry abounds while they are in France."

>"The problem is that her time lies some way in the future. Isabelle of Valois, daughter of Charles VI of France, was born in the Louvre in Paris on November 9, 1389. She is not quite seven years old"

###### Imagine being born into a royal family, where you are living in better conditions than most peasants, and this is the fate you get stuck with.

>pg. 21
>"He spends huge sums with mercers and tailors and always dressed magnificently — today he wears scarlet; on another it will be white and green; on a third he may appear clad in all gold. All of this leave a deep impression on Henry, as his later life will show."



### Chapter 3: BEHOLD, I WILL OPEN YOUR GRAVES

###### In this chapter Henry will learn what an enraged king is willing to do to those that humiliated him. Richard II saw his mentor die due to a deranged council that stripped him of his power when he was only 10.

>pg. 26
>"Bolingbroke joined a faction of noblemen who took the teenaged King Richard to task for the inadequacies of his kingship"


### Chapter 4: ROUGH JUSTICE

###### This is after the appellants (Another name for the council who defied Richard and sentenced his mentor to death) are themselves sentenced to death. Henry IV escapes death.
