###### First of all, I want to say that the Symposium is truly a work of art by Plato, about a few philosophers getting together to figure out what love is, where it comes from, and the true nature of it. It was truly a ride for me. Short but sweet



## [[Phaedrus]]' Speech


>pg. 10.
>The every first of all gods she devised was love. So there's wide assent that Love is a primordial god.



>pg. 11
>Anyone who wants to live a good life needs to be guided throughout his life by something which love imparts more effectively than family ties can, or public office, or wealth, or anything else.


###### What [[Phaedrus]] will do is describe love as transcendent of all material things; such as wealth or family ties.


> pg. 11
> possession by Love would infuse even utter cowards with courage and make them indistinguishable from those to whom bravery comes easily.


###### The act of being in love he explains, is something that goes to blind fears and makes cowards rise up in the face of danger.


###### This is truly a beautiful thing. When you truly love a person or a thing, you are willing to sacrifice your life for it, because without it, life means nothing. The love that you give; the constant search for knowledge, for you other half, is the reason to keep living.



>pg. 12
>The reason was that although Achilles found out from his mother that his killing of Hector would cause his own death, and that if he avoided doing this he'd go home and die of old age, he was brave enough to stand by his lover Patroclus and to avenge him - he didn't choose just to die for Patroclus, but even to die as well as him



###### This will all relate to the [[The Iliad]], where we will see these events play out with [[Achilles]], [[Hector]], and [[Patroclus]]


###### What [[Phaedrus]] argues is that true love is sacrifice for someone else, or for a greater good. I can relate this to the show [[One Piece]], where [[Hiriluk]] is willing to die to treat patients. His calling ultimately is helping people. People usually think of love as the act of loving another person, but as people later will explain, love is more complicated than that. Love can involve the love of wisdom, the love of a certain subject. There are no bounds to what love can entail.



## [[Pausania]]'s Speech

>pg. 14
>The same goes for loving and for Love, then: only the Love who incites us to love property is good and  deserves our praise


###### [[Pausania]] will shift in another direction, basically saying that the only kind of love that is right and just is the one that comes from a person who is just that; wise, right, and just.



###### To him, there are two kinds of love: Celestial Love and Common Love. Here are some examples of both:


#### Examples of Common Love:

>pg. 14
>In the first place, they love women as well as boys; secondly, when they do fall in love, there attracted to the bodies rather than the minds of the people they love; thirdly, the reason they're attracted to the most unintelligent people imaginable is that all they're after is the satisfaction of their desires and the don't care whether or not those desires are properly satisfied.



###### You see this today even in the 21th century. People want to experience love without the hard work that is involved. When he talks about people pursuing the kind of love in order to quench their desires, I immediately think of hookup culture, where the point of the relationship is to satisfy the physical desires of the body.


###### I agree that this is not a love to strive for. This does not last. As will later be mentioned by [[Socrates]], the true end of the road is finding beauty itself. People would rather satisfy their physical desires than work towards something immortal that will outlive them; the kind of love that passes on generation after generation.


#### Examples of Celestial Love:

>pg. 14
>On the other hand, the Love who accompanies Celestial Aphrodite is wholly male, with no trace of femininity. That is why this Love's inspiration makes people feel affection for what is inherently stronger and more intelligent



###### Not sure what he means by "wholly male".  It seems that back then, there was a different notion of love between men. This was sort of like a mentorship where the younger man sought out the older man to learn more about the world. I'll have to come back to this,


#### Back to the main speech:

>pg. 17
>A lover is bad if he is of the common type, who loves the body rather than the mind. This makes him inconstant, because there's no constancy in the object of his desires; as soo as the physical bloom that attracted him fades, he "flies away and is gone".


###### This is true. What happens when the person you loved for their body ages and gets all wrinkly. The physical bloom that attracted you disappears. That's why you must search for a deeper, more fulfilling love with someone.


>pg. 17
>On the other hand, a lover who loves goodness of character is constant for life, because of the constancy of the object he's been united with



###### Exactly. Loving someone for their personality will never get old. This is something that rides with you until the day of death.


>pg. 18
>We sanction... well, just as we found that it was acceptable, without being obsequious or reprehensible, for lovers to do  anything, however servile, for their boyfriends, so by the same token there's only one form of voluntary slavery left which isn't reprehensible


###### This is kind of a leap. So what I'm getting is to show that you are truly committed to someone, you must in a way commit to a sort of acceptable form of slavery where you do anything for them.



###### In a way, I kind of see his point. We are all slaves to love. We all feel like we must do something because of it, such as die for your lover.


>pg. 19
>So there's absolutely nothing wrong with gratifying a lover for the sake of virtue. This is the Love which belongs with the Celestial goddess


###### So the main point made is that one must strive for the Celestial kind of love, rather than a Common type of love, where the only thing holding people together is the desire for the physical needs, such as sex, which is something that is not constant for life, as when people age, they become a different person physically.
