## What is Metaphysics?

###### My understanding: Metaphysics is essentially how we can know what we know. For example, what are the properties of certain objects, such as an apple or a human being? What is the bare minimum necessary for something to "be"?


### Substance

#### This is essentially something that exists in itself

- A dog
- A tree
- A human being


### Accident
#### This is something that exists in a substance

#### For example, some accidents for an apple would be the following:
- The color is red
- The shape is circular
- it weights 100 grams


###### So does every substance contain an accident? Lets think about it; for a human, the accidents would be things such as height, eye color, skin color, our opinions. We are all partaking in the substance of being human, but our accidents are much different depending on the person

###### Can an accident exist without the substance? Well things such as size can exist in different substances. For example, both humans and a dog can have an accident weight, and at times have the same substance such as having the same color tongue.


###### [[Transubstantiation]] will basically embody the idea of the substance staying the same but the accidents changing.  For example, the bread and the wine literally turn into the body and the blood of [[Jesus Christ]], even if the substance will still look the same.

###### So if Catholics are to accept this doctrine, then are there other things that can change their accidents but still have the same property??? By this logic, why do Catholics object to a woman being able to become a man? If their substance stays the same but their accident changes, why under this worldview would this be rejected?




## Act

#### This is essentially what something is. It sounds like substance.
###### My way of distinguishing between act and substance is that the act is the current state of the substance. For example a butterfly would be the actual substance. The nature of the butterfly will never change in all of its stages, but the act is the current state that it is in i.e. if it is in a cocoon or if it is fully grown.  


## Potency

#### The potential that something has. Meaning what it can become

###### For example, an ice cube can become liquid. It has the potency to do that just as a human being has the potential to be rotted. Or the same way the an apple has the potential to rot and to go bad. When this happens, the substance of the apple would remain the same, but the accidents would be different from an apple that is not rotted, as the apple that is not rotted would still be red, and the rotted one would turn more brown. The act can be described as the state of being good to eat, or to be rotted. Those would be the states that we would describe the apple to be in.
